About Senarath Bank

A fictional Sri Lankan bank built for security simulation.

Founded in 1998, Senarath Bank PLC has grown from a single branch in Colombo into a trusted island-wide bank serving over 240,000 customers. We provide personal and business banking, lending, cards and digital banking through our SenNet platform.

This institution is entirely fictional. It exists only as a controlled, deliberately vulnerable environment for cybersecurity training and SIEM detection testing. No real money, accounts, or customers are involved.

Our mission

To make secure, modern banking accessible to every Sri Lankan — from the first rupee saved to the growth of a family business.
